# Character artwork

Generated specifically for Karachi Run. Transparent PNGs are used in the menu, chase indicator, crash screen, only. In-game heads and full bodies now use 3D geometry, without pasted portrait planes.

- `public/characters/imran-khan.png`: animated Imran Khan portrait.
- `public/characters/asim-munir.png`: animated Asim Munir portrait.

Shared generation brief: Single game character bust for Karachi Run, a clearly fictional political satire runner. Original polished rounded animated 3D mobile-game rendering, appealing expressive proportions, recognizable facial likeness. Square centered chest-up bust, front facing with slight three-quarter turn, entire head and hair or cap visible with comfortable top margin, crisp readable silhouette at 64px. Soft warm studio lighting. Transparent alpha background. No lettering, logos, UI, borders, watermarks, or weapons.

Imran brief: swept dark graying hair, mature angular face, clean-shaven, cream-white shalwar kameez and forest-green waistcoat; confident, friendly, determined expression.

Asim brief: thick dark moustache, mature face and dark hair, fictional khaki chase-officer uniform with simple rank detail and peaked cap; stern, comically determined expression.

City geometry, landmark interpretations, vehicles, pickups, and Urdu sign textures are built in the project. Landmarks are stylized and the route is compressed for gameplay. No Subway Surfers assets are used.

The current in-game characters are the user's supplied `model.glb` files from `public/imran.zip` and `public/asim.zip`. The project retains both archives unchanged. Optimized browser copies live in `public/models/`. Their source meshes contain no skeleton or animation clips, so the game fits an 18-joint humanoid rig and drives it with the shared procedural fallback poses. Imran’s topology-smoothed arm weights are generated by `node scripts/prepare-scan-arm-weights.mjs` into `public/models/imran-arm-weights.bin`; regenerate them if his optimized GLB changes. The original scans and earlier arm mask are retained. These supplied assets replace the earlier procedural character bodies; their external authorship and licensing are not asserted here.

## Location photographs consulted for the September 12 rebuild

Photos were inspected as modeling references; no photo was copied into the game. Models are stylized interpretations, not measured scans.

- Teen Talwar: https://www.hotel7seven.com/teen-talwar.jpg — three parallel blades, curved connected guard forms, low traffic island.
- Mazar-e-Quaid: https://tourisminpakistan.com/storage/23/01J8MENWFR1RYD3EM2ERS4V4YT.jpg — tapered marble walls, single tall pointed arch per elevation, hemispherical dome, terrace.
- Empress Market: https://thekarachiwalla.com/2010/10/05/karachi-landmarks-the-empress-market/ — sandstone arcades, red hipped roofs, clock tower and end pavilions.
- Merewether Tower: https://heritage.eftsindh.com/districts/karachi/merewether-tower.php — Gothic gables, paired louvres, rose tracery, pinnacles and slender spire.
- Mazda W-11 minibus: https://www.thenews.com.pk/tns/detail/1234839-waris-khan-minibus-driver — sloped front, decorated side panels, chrome stripes and ornamental roof rack.

The bus floral texture is original canvas artwork. Current in-game facial features and clothing come directly from the supplied GLBs.

## Approved Karachi Run brand artwork

The original generated logo and squircle icon are in `art/branding/` with their generation briefs. Production exports in `public/branding/` and `public/favicon.ico` are deterministic size/format derivatives created by `scripts/prepare-branding.mjs`. The source artwork is retained unchanged. The maskable icon uses a plain emerald full-bleed background and keeps the complete wordmark inside the platform-safe central area.

## Haris sprint animation

Haris uses the sprint clip from swift502/Sketchbook, retargeted offline onto his existing skeleton. Copyright (c) 2020 swift502, MIT licensed. The source GLB and license are retained in art/motion; the 30 KB generated rotation data is bundled into the game. Distribution includes public/licenses/sketchbook.txt and offline caching of that notice. See art/motion/README.md for source hashes, regeneration and scope. No Sketchbook character geometry is used in the game.
