# Character artwork

Generated specifically for Karachi Run. Transparent PNGs are used in the menu, chase indicator, crash screen, and as face textures on original procedural 3D bodies.

- `public/characters/imran-khan.png`: animated Imran Khan portrait.
- `public/characters/asim-munir.png`: animated Asim Munir portrait.

Shared generation brief: Single game character bust for Karachi Run, a clearly fictional political satire runner. Original polished rounded animated 3D mobile-game rendering, appealing expressive proportions, recognizable facial likeness. Square centered chest-up bust, front facing with slight three-quarter turn, entire head and hair or cap visible with comfortable top margin, crisp readable silhouette at 64px. Soft warm studio lighting. Transparent alpha background. No lettering, logos, UI, borders, watermarks, or weapons.

Imran brief: swept dark graying hair, mature angular face, clean-shaven, cream-white shalwar kameez and forest-green waistcoat; confident, friendly, determined expression.

Asim brief: thick dark moustache, mature face and dark hair, fictional khaki chase-officer uniform with simple rank detail and peaked cap; stern, comically determined expression.

All city geometry, landmark interpretations, characters' articulated bodies, vehicles, pickups, and Urdu sign textures are built in the project. Landmarks are stylized and the route is compressed for gameplay. These are original assets, not Subway Surfers assets.
