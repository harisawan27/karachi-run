## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: original primary title wordmark for KARACHI RUN, a polished mobile-first 3D endless-runner game set in Karachi, Pakistan. This is a finished game-brand artwork concept, not a UI screen or corporate identity presentation.
Primary request: Create ONE exceptional, commercially polished, exuberant arcade-game logo. The broad genre reference is the bold dimensional lettering and immediate readability of well-known mobile runner branding, but design an original visual identity and original letterforms for Karachi Run. Give this game a memorable Pakistani truck-art / Karachi street-art personality.
Text, verbatim, and the ONLY words: "KARACHI" above "RUN". Spell K-A-R-A-C-H-I accurately; RUN has exactly three letters.
Composition: centered tightly connected two-line wordmark, approximately 3:2 overall silhouette, generously large in a landscape canvas with about 8 percent transparent padding. KARACHI is a broad arching top line, RUN is a massive powerful lower line, both unmistakably readable at phone sizes. Hand-drawn chunky block letters, playful forward energy, confident uneven baseline, thick sculpted bevels, tactile painted faces, compact dark petrol-green extrusion, crisp cream outer keyline. Clear separation between all letters and especially between the two As.
Color: KARACHI has rich warm mango-gold / tangerine painted faces and cream edge highlights; RUN has saturated emerald and turquoise faces with warm lime-gold bevel highlights. Deep petrol-green shadows unify the silhouette; restrained magenta and bright turquoise truck-art accents provide a signature. Make the typography the star.
Pakistani personality: very restrained authentic hand-painted truck-art floral curls tucked into two outer corners of the lettering; three energetic paint flecks / speed strokes. A SMALL elegant white dome silhouette suggesting Mazar-e-Quaid can peek subtly above the center of the top word, secondary to the text. Keep the central counter spaces open, with no decorative detail obscuring the title.
Style: premium stylized 3D mobile-game title art, crisp silhouette, smooth hand-painted rendering, lively controlled shapes, directional upper-left highlights, excellent depth, a coherent finished brand rather than generic AI ornament.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha; no background scene, no square tile, no backdrop color, no checkerboard baked into the artwork, no floor, no ambient drop-shadow rectangle.
Constraints: original design; no Subway Surfers wording, no copied letterforms or existing logo shapes; no characters, no trains, no politicians; no other text, slogans, watermark or mockups; no small unreadable lettering; no minimal K monogram, SaaS icon or corporate geometry. Produce one logo, not a sheet of options.
